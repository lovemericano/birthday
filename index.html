A Letter

Dear Restu,

I've been trying to figure out how to write this for a while now. There's no clean way to put years of something into words, but I'll try.

You were my first love. I don't think I fully understood what that meant when we were younger, but I understand it now. First love isn't just about the feelings — it's about the version of yourself you were when you met that person. You were part of mine.

When we went our separate ways, I didn't disappear from the world. I kept going, kept meeting people, kept building my life. But somewhere in the middle of all that, I noticed something. Every time I got close to someone new, I'd find myself searching. Not intentionally. But I'd catch myself noticing a personality trait, a way of laughing, a quiet habit — and thinking, that reminds me of her.

I never said it out loud. But I kept looking, and I never really found it. Because it was yours. Just yours.

And then 2026 happened. Somehow, after all of it, we found our way back to each other. I didn't see it coming, but when it did, it felt less like a surprise and more like something that had been quietly waiting.

I'm not writing this to romanticize the past or pretend things were perfect. I'm writing it because on your 24th birthday, I want you to know that I'm genuinely grateful. Grateful you exist. Grateful that I get to be part of your story again.

You deserve good things, Restu. More than you probably give yourself credit for.

Happy birthday.

— yours.
